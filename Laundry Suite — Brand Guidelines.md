# Laundry Suite — Brand Guidelines

> **Satu Platform untuk Mengelola Seluruh Bisnis Laundry**

---

## 1. Brand Overview

### Brand Name

**Laundry Suite**

### Brand Category

B2B SaaS / Business Management Platform

### Industry

Laundry & Textile Care

### Target Market

Bisnis laundry di Indonesia:

- Laundry rumahan
- Laundry kiloan
- Laundry satuan
- Laundry premium
- Laundry multi-outlet
- Laundry franchise
- Jaringan laundry skala enterprise

### Primary Positioning

> **Laundry Suite adalah all-in-one business operating suite yang membantu pemilik laundry mengelola operasional, pelanggan, karyawan, delivery, inventory, dan keuangan dalam satu platform.**

### Short Positioning

> **The Operating Suite for Laundry Businesses**

# 11. Product Naming Convention

```text

├── People
```

